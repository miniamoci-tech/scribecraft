## Progress log
- 2026-10-25 — The interface now speaks Italian as well as English. Every user-visible string is in `crates/ui-egui/src/i18n.rs` with both translations in one table, so a string added without its translation fails to compile; tests check both languages use the same placeholders and that nothing is left in English. A selector in the toolbar switches at once and the choice is written atomically to `ui.json` beside the job list (a missing or damaged file means English, never a failure to start). Job states, priorities, channels, transport states, the Pedal and keys window, hints and reports are all translated; messages the engine, the platform or a device produce stay in their own language. The longer Italian buttons exposed a layout defect: the bookmark field and its two buttons on one line ran 41 pt past the details panel, and a resizable panel grows to fit its content, so it crept to its maximum and clipped every label. The buttons now sit under the field, and a headless test holds the row inside the panel and the panel at one width in both languages. Next: phase 4 (pedal learning wizard), then phase 5 (light theme).
- 2026-10-25 — Picking a dictation in the queue now opens it and starts playback from where it was left; the "Add recordings…" dialog reports how many were added and opens the first of the batch, with the tooltip saying several can be picked at once. Phase 4 (pedal learning wizard) next.
- 2026-10-25 — Restyle phase 3: the dictation's fields moved out of the scrolling centre into a resizable details panel on the right, with a status pill in its header, labelled fields that line up, and the bookmark list with jump links, an add field and a copy-time action. The centre now holds the recording card and a large notes editor, where the words are actually written. The panel scrolls on its own. `apply_edits` was extracted and tested: a field edit or a paste leaves bookmarks, the path and the saved position alone. Phase 4 (pedal learning wizard) next.
- 2026-10-25 — Restyle phase 2: the queue has filter chips (All / To do / In progress / Done / Sent) with live counts that keep only the matching jobs, and hand-painted rows with a red or amber priority stripe, an elided title, status · author and a right-aligned duration. Metadata stays on WCAG-AA colours on hover and on the open row. A headless test clicks the Done chip and checks the queue narrows to finished work. Phase 3 (details panel) next.
- 2026-10-25 — Restyle phase 1: one dark palette and widget style in `theme.rs`, contrast-checked by tests; the transport moved into a card dock at the bottom with a large monospaced clock, a state pill that names the scan rate, time remaining, bookmark marks on the seek bar and an accent playhead. The open dictation's pane scrolls on short windows. Phase 2 (job-list filters and chips) next.
- 2026-10-25 — Fix: the transport bar ended any scan not backed by its own button or F8, so a fast-forward started by a foot pedal or a system-wide key stopped after one frame (play was unaffected). The bar now ends only a scan it started; holding ⏪/⏩ scans and a tap skips by the configured amount. Pedal learning waits until every pedal is released and refuses a bit another pedal already uses, so releasing a pedal held while Learn was clicked no longer stores a duplicate. First headless UI tests (transport bar).
- 2026-10-20 — M3: foot pedals on USB (HID, learned with one press) and serial COM ports (NCH public wiring defaults), system-wide hotkeys (F7/F8 hold, F9, F4, F6; remappable) that work while the word processor is in front; `scribecraft-cli pedal list|monitor`; pedal automation tools. Checked by hand on Windows 10; no physical pedal yet.
- 2026-10-19 — 0.1.1: restored the `cargo xtask` alias (#1); CI green on Windows, macOS and Linux; first packaged release (setup .exe, .dmg, tar.gz) from the release workflow.
- 2026-10-19 — M0: workspace, WSOLA constant-pitch speed, Symphonia decode with limits, transport (backstep, held rewind/FF, presets), job list with resume and bookmarks, 21 automation tools with `--root` confinement, CLI with real audio output, xtask gates (layers, assets, parity, wasm, ci, dist). M2.0: first desktop window, checked by hand on Windows 10.
